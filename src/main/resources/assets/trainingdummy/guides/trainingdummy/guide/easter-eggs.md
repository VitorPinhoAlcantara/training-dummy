# Easter Eggs

A few dummy nicknames (and one item trick) do something special. Nicknames aren't case-sensitive.

---

## Sound-only nicknames

These dummies play a custom hit sound instead of the default one.

- Topiinho
- Danrique
- MitinhoPlayer
- JazaraGamer
- Nofaxu
- mamao170
- MeioElfo
- BrunimNeets

## Nicknames that do more

### Immortal

Rename a dummy "Immortal" and place it down. The name might be a little misleading.

<br />

### Herobrine

Everyone knows you don't name a dummy "Herobrine". Don't be the one who finds out why.

<br />

### Jack o'Lantern

Some dummies just really need a Jack o'Lantern for a head. Try handing one over.
